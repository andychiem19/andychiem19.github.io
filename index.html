About me

I'm an undergraduate studying Electrical and Computer Engineering at Cornell University with a focus on FPGA/ASIC engineering and applications. My project and research work has centered on the implementation/optimization of complex math in hardware, from CORDIC-based options pricing to Ising-model optimization.

I recently completed a co-op at Woodward McCoach Inc., where I validated high-speed serial interfaces, worked on production telecommunications hardware, and developed 100G Ethernet verification software.
