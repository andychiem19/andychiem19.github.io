Problem

A new addition to the IP library, our AXI-Lite Performance Monitor, had recently been tested on a variety of our boards' AXI-Lite interfaces. The performance monitor had returned reasonable test results on all our boards, except one (let's call it board X), where it was reading about 70% of the expected bandwidth.

This could mean one of two things:

  1. Board X's AXI-Lite interface was genuinely running at 70% of the rated bandwidth
  2. An issue exists in the Performance Monitor IP that only emerged on one board

Preliminary testing revealed that data wasn't being lost on board X's AXI-Lite interface, so it was most likely running at expected bandwidth, leading me to focus on the second possibility.

Investigation

I started with hardware testing through our software for controlling the boards, running sets of commands to configure the snapshot interface and simulate an AXI-Lite link for long enough to monitor it over a given snapshot size.

On 4G PRBS testing, I replicated the previous results after inserting and building board X with the performance monitor embedded.

However, when I moved to the 50G 100GAUI test that had returned lower than expected results, I got unreasonably low bandwidth numbers, even lower than prior tests had found, in the range of 10% to 50% efficiency. Prior testing had shown about 60%, while expected efficiency was about 83%.
